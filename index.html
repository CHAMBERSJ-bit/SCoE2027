Learning Experiences from Experienced Learners

Delivered in partnership with the University of Melbourne and the Victorian Government, the STEM Centre of Excellence (SCoE) at Science Gallery Melbourne investigates the collision of art and science. Embracing scientific and artistic inquiry alike, our programs, designed in collaboration with and for young people, foster development of transferable future-focused skills and explore design thinking programs through a creative lens.

Science Gallery Melbourne

Science Gallery Melbourne seeks to involve, inspire and transform curious minds through art and science. Located in Melbourne Connect, the University of Melbourne’s hub for innovation, we are part of the International Science Gallery Network connecting creative thinkers worldwide. Through exhibitions, events, learning experiences, and research, the gallery doesn’t just share knowledge — it shapes the next generation of science and technology trailblazers. It is a space to challenge ideas, experiment boldly, and ask: what’s next?

Build Your Experience

Mix and match your visit

Our programs for secondary-aged learners are available as single workshops, or as extended experiences by pairing them with another workshop or gallery tour to curate an exciting day that fits your schedule. Our curriculum-connected programs link to multiple Learning areas and Capabilities, and our educators differentiate content and delivery to suit the level and needs of the group. Using an interdisciplinary approach program design, we relate the evolving content of our future-focused exhibitions to our school learning experiences, enabling teachers and students to make relevant connections.

More than a day out

Our workshops for STEM Centre of Excellence Partner Schools are designed to enhance school-based learning. We work with your team to curate a selection of visits to achieve desired learning outcomes. Each Partner School receives access to a set number of visits each year based on our resourcing. How you use these visits is up to you! You may choose to use them for a whole year level as part of a unit of work, or for a smaller group of students coming on repeated visits.

Exhibition Overviews

RADICAL CARE: Kinship Collab

RADICAL CARE: Kinship Collab exhibition is a major University of Melbourne collaboration between BLAK C.O.R.E. and Science Gallery Melbourne. RADICAL CARE asks what becomes possible when kinship leads, centres and shapes how we think about Indigenous ways of being. Bringing together global Indigenous artists, cultural practitioners and knowledge holders, the Collab creates space for Indigenous methodologies, innovation, experimentation and exchange.

SENSUAL

Experience seeps in through the senses. Ensembles of sensory neurons are constantly channeling information to our brains for processing. SENSUAL seeks to tweak the sensorial streams we are tuning into, shifting the mix to amplify palpable qualities of perception. It’s getting harder to believe what we see, so how can we build trust in what we feel? Out of sight, out of mind, and into the body – come together and probe the rich sensory layer between our inner world and the external environment.

Special Programs

One Ocean Special Program: SCoE Schools

Wednesday 24 February 2027
10:00am to 2:30pm

One Ocean is a full-day experience that connects Indigenous knowledge with climate change.

Students open the day at the One Ocean concert, where artists from nations across the Indo-Pacific bring live music and film together on stage. They share their musical traditions and languages, and show how the climate crisis is reaching their island homes.

After the concert, students rotate through three kinds of activity: artist-led experiences of the RADICAL CARE: Kinship Collab exhibition, hands-on workshops on Indigenous knowledge, and in-conversation sessions led by the Wurundjeri Woi-wurrung Cultural Heritage Aboriginal Corporation.

Careers Discovery Day

Tuesday 31 August 2027
Delivered at Science Gallery Melbourne and Melbourne Connect
Years 9–10

Our Careers Discovery Day invites students in Years 9–10 to meet people whose career pathway looks like a squiggle, not a straight line.

Learn about wonderful opportunities that blend creativity with critical thinking, science with art, among others.

Focusing on exciting future professions, students will:

  • Gain career advice directly from people in the know
  • Participate in a live Future Careers Forum, with the audience streamed to schools Australia-wide and internationally
  • Learn more about emerging STEAM (jobs that integrate Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Maths) careers
  • Have fun participating in the Human Library – borrow someone for a minute!
  • Explore industry partners’ workplaces in the Gallery
